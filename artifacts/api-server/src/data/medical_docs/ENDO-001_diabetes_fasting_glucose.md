# Fasting Blood Glucose & HbA1c Reference Standards
Document ID: ENDO-001
Source: ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes
Publisher: American Diabetes Association
Document Type: clinical_guideline
Version: 2026.1

## Glycemic Thresholds
Normal fasting plasma glucose is 70 to 99 mg/dL. Impaired fasting glucose (prediabetes threshold) is 100 to 125 mg/dL. Fasting plasma glucose >=126 mg/dL on two separate occasions confirms diabetes mellitus.

## Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) Ranges
Normal HbA1c level is <5.7%. Prediabetes HbA1c range is 5.7% to 6.4%. Diabetes mellitus diagnosis is established at HbA1c >=6.5%.
